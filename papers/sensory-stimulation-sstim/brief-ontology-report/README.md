## Publication integrity

This is **the first complete manuscript version for author review**, not an accepted or submitted paper. It is not correct to claim final author approval, current independent validation, or that the article meets every production requirement before those steps have occurred. The existing release examples are synthetic or literature-derived; the article does not describe them as new human-subject data.

### Essential pre-submission gates (not satisfied by writing alone)

1. **Author review and authorship:** The corresponding author must review every technical and bibliographic statement, decide contributors/authors on the basis of actual contributions, and approve the final author list and affiliation. No named prospective collaborator may be added without consent.
2. **Evidence of added value:** For Applied Ontology, reviewers may demand stronger comparison against OBI and a reproducible independent representation use case. The existing qualitative examples are a credible starting point, not proof of superiority.
3. **Pinned release checks:** Re-run and archive the exact release validation in a suitable environment. No claims of tests personally executed during preparation of this report are made.
4. **Bibliography/style:** Verify every author list and journal citation against authoritative metadata. The draft includes expanded APA-style citations but has not undergone a publisher-format/typesetting check.
5. **Declarations:** Verify funding, competing interests, affiliation, AI disclosure (including past AI use on the codebase where relevant), and author-contribution wording. Confirm any confidential or personal material is excluded.
6. **Journal submission form:** Prepare a separate Sage Royal single-column LaTeX/Word submission from this canonical manuscript; ensure author contact data, figures and metadata match.
7. **No W3C endorsement claim:** The W3C Community Group is an open forum, not a W3C Recommendation or publisher of this scholarly paper.
