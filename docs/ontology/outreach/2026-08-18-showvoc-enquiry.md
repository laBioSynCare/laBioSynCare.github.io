Hello,

I maintain SSTIM, the Sensory Stimulation Ontology — an OWL 2 ontology with a
multilingual SKOS vocabulary (en/it/pt/es) and SHACL shapes, for describing
sensory stimulation techniques, protocols, exposure conditions, observations,
safety metadata and evidence-qualified claims. It is CC BY 4.0, has stable w3id
identifiers with content negotiation, and each release is archived on Zenodo.

  https://w3id.org/sstim
  https://labiosyncare.github.io/ontology/docs/

I would like to ask before doing anything, rather than submit and find out.

1. Does the Publications Office instance at https://showvoc.op.europa.eu/ accept
   datasets from outside the EU institutions? Looking at the hosted projects,
   the large majority are data.europa.eu, publications.europa.eu and INSPIRE,
   with a small number of external references such as OBO ontologies, GeoNames
   and the NCI Thesaurus. It was not obvious to me whether that short list
   reflects an open policy with a high bar, or a closed one with specific
   exceptions.

2. If it does, is the in-app contribution workflow the right route, or should
   the request go to the Publications Office directly? I could see that ShowVoc
   supports contribution requests, but not whether that path is enabled for the
   public on that particular instance.

3. If the EU instance is not the right home, is there a community ShowVoc
   instance where a vocabulary like this would be more appropriate?

Happy to provide anything useful — SKOS and OWL statistics, the SHACL shapes, or
a specific distribution file. SSTIM is currently 551 SKOS concepts across 68
concept schemes plus 164 OWL classes, and it is already catalogued in BARTOC,
FAIRsharing and BioPortal, so I can supply metadata in whatever form is easiest.
